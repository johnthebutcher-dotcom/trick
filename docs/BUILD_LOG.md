## 2026-10-23 — Four-colour suits, accessibility, sounds (+ joker), table fits the screen

- **Built:** four owner-requested additions, all driven by new `Prefs` fields (`fourColour`, `dyslexia`, `contrast`, `soundMode`) mirrored onto `<html>` by `applyAppearance` the same way `applyHandSize` already was. Old stored prefs (which had `sound: boolean`, unused) merge forward cleanly; `sound` was replaced by `soundMode`.
  - **Four-colour suits** (Settings → Suit colours: Two/Four). Each suit inks itself through a `suit-ink-{S,H,D,C}` class; four-colour is a single token swap under `:root[data-suits='four']` — clubs green (`--suit-green`), diamonds blue (`--suit-blue`), spades black, hearts red. Applied in `CardChip`, `HandView` and the bidding box.
  - **Dyslexia-friendly text** (`data-font='readable'`): Atkinson Hyperlegible (added to the Google Fonts link) with looser letter/word spacing and line-height; headings included while on.
  - **High contrast** (`data-contrast='high'`): overrides card-room ink, borders and the three suit colours harder, and thickens card borders. Keeps the felt.
  - **Sounds** (Off / Gentle / Joker) in `features/play/sound.ts`, fired from the drill answer tap (`playAnswer(correct, mode)`). Gentle = soft chime / soft tone; **joker = a random fart on a wrong answer, a random cheer (applause / crowd / slide-whistle) on a right one** — the owner's mum's request. All nine WAVs were **synthesised** (numpy) into `public/sounds/`; no real clips (the sandbox can't fetch audio) — owner can send real clips to swap in. Default is Off. Added `webp` + `wav` to the PWA `globPatterns` so wood, felt and the sounds precache for offline.
- **Play table fits the screen:** opponents (N/E/W) are now compact backs + a count instead of a full row; seat labels smaller; gaps tighter; bid buttons `h-11`. The page is two flex columns: the table + your hand on one side, the bidding box + coach on the other. In portrait they stack (your hand above the bidding box, so her cards always show); in landscape they sit side by side, and your hand is lifted to the top of its column (`landscape:order-first`) so it stays on-screen next to the bidding box.
- **Decided:** bid chips stay on cards (never wood), so four-colour and suit contrast hold. Dyslexia font uses Atkinson Hyperlegible (reliable via Google Fonts, built for legibility) rather than OpenDyslexic (not on Google Fonts); can swap if she prefers that specific look. Sounds only fire in drills (right/wrong there); Play has no per-move right/wrong. Appearance prefs apply after load like text size (one brief flash on cold start, not mirrored pre-paint).
- **Left out:** real recorded joker clips (synthesised for now); per-suit custom colour choice; a landscape layout for the full play phase beyond what the two columns already give; still no first-name greeting (owner hasn't given it) and still don't know which system her class uses.
- **Checked:** `npm run check` green (54 tests, build). Screenshots at 390px portrait and 844×420 landscape, dark + light, with four-colour and high-contrast on: suit colours, the compact table, the two-column landscape (hand + bidding box both visible) and the Settings controls all correct.
- **Status:** pushed to `main`.

## 2026-10-22 — 2-over-1 content pack (third system; integrated with the Acol work)

- **Built:** 2-over-1 as a third bidding system, un-greyed in Settings. It opens exactly like Standard American, so `systems/two-over-one.ts` re-uses SAYC's opening functions and differs only in responding. The responding ladder for a 1-of-a-major opening moved into `systems/responses.ts` (`respondToMajor(hand, opener, variant)`, `isTeachableResponder`, `responseOptions`); `RuleModule` gained one field, `respondingVariant` (`'standard'` for SAYC and Acol, `'two-over-one'` for 2/1), which is the single source of truth both the drill and the bot read. New skill **`respond-1-suit`** ("Responding to 1 of a suit"), added to `SYSTEM_SPECIFIC`, so its ids are `system:respond-1-suit:n` and each system keeps its own ladder. The bot/hint (`play/auction.ts advise`) now reads `respondingVariant`: a two-level new suit needs 10+ in the standard systems but 13+ (game forcing) in 2/1, with the in-between hands routed to a forcing 1NT. Cheat-sheet gained a 2/1 "responding to a suit" section (`TWO_OVER_ONE_SHEET` = SAYC's sheet with that one section swapped). 6 new tests (54 total): `two-over-one.test.ts` plus a table-bot divergence case in `play.test.ts`.
- **The one rule 2/1 changes (at this level):** a new suit at the two level shows 10+ in SAYC/Acol but **forces to game** (≈13+) in 2/1; hands too good to pass but short of game bid a **forcing 1NT** and show the suit next. Everything below that — pass, the simple raise, a one-level new suit, the catch-all 1NT — is identical and lives in the shared `responses.ts` ladder. The drill constrains the two-level suit to diamonds so each hand has one clean answer; the fixed option set is five per opener (e.g. over 1♥: Pass / 1♠ / 1NT / 2♥ / 2♦). Openings are asserted byte-for-byte identical to SAYC in a test. Diagnostic over 48 drill hands: under SAYC the invitational `2♦` hands, under 2/1 become forcing `1NT`, so roughly one hand per eight-hand drill visibly differs.
- **Integration note (important for the next session):** this was built in parallel with the Acol commit (`cd4c9d3`) — two sessions independently made the system "real" at the same minute, touching the same ~15 files with different shapes (this branch had named things `BiddingSystem`/`getSystem`; Acol's, which landed on `main` first, used `RuleModule`/`rulesFor`). On the owner's say-so, 2/1 was rebased onto the Acol base and conformed to **its** naming and id scheme (shared skills unprefixed `skill:n`, system-specific `system:skill:n`), not the other way round. So the two efforts are now one registry with all three systems; nothing Acol was discarded.
- **Decided / scoped:** `respond-1-suit` covers responses to **1♥/1♠ only** (where 2/1 is defined and the forcing 1NT lives); minors and the rest of the ladder are later skills. Raises to the three/four level and 4+-support game hands are filtered out of this skill (a "raising partner" lesson). `open-pass`/`opening-bid` are keyed per-system even though 2/1 shares SAYC's openings, so a learner who switched SAYC→2/1 would re-grind those two drills (the content is identical) — acceptable because a learner picks one system and stays; a per-(skill,system) canonical mapping would remove even that, if it ever matters. Acol responds to a major the standard way, so it reuses the `'standard'` variant; its signature stays the weak 1NT in the openings.
- **Left out:** 2/1 responses to a minor opening; Stayman/transfers (still SAYC-shaped in the bot/sheet); limit-raise gadgets; skills 5–8 from the roadmap. Nothing new in the bot's rebid logic (it already always bids again over a 1NT response, which is consistent with a forcing 1NT).
- **Design pass:** no new UI or effects — the responding drill reuses `DrillPage` and the cheat-sheet reuses `SheetPage`; `check:effects` unchanged. Settings radio and the ladder already render from data, so 2/1 and the new skill appear with no layout work.
- **Needs the owner:** still worth asking which system her class uses (open question in SPEC). All three are selectable now.
- **Status:** full `npm run check` green (lint, typecheck, format, check:effects, 54 tests, build); pushed to `main`.

## 2026-10-22 — Acol content pack (bidding system is now real, not greyed out)

- **Built:** Acol as a second bidding system. A system is now a rule module behind a registry (`features/bridge/systems/`: `types.ts` the `RuleModule`/`SystemId` contract, `index.ts` the `SYSTEMS` list + `rulesFor(id)`, `sayc.ts` and `acol.ts` each exporting a `rules` object). `acol.ts` encodes the beginner differences: four-card majors (open 1♥/1♠ with four; 1♥ when 4-4), a WEAK 1NT of 12–14, 2NT 20–22, prepared 1♣ with equal minors; same rule-of-20 and point counting as SAYC. The drill engine (`skills.ts`), scheduler and the bot/hint (`play/auction.ts advise`) now take the active system and dispatch through `rulesFor`; the 1NT responder derives invite/game thresholds from the system's NT range instead of a hardcoded 15–17. Cheat-sheet is per-system (`ACOL_SHEET` in `sheet.ts`, `SheetPage` reads `prefs.system`). SettingsPage un-greys ACOL automatically (the registry marks it `available`). `useGame`/`PlayPage`/`DrillPage`/`TodayPage` thread `prefs.system` through. 6 Acol tests + 1 scheduler test (41 total).
- **Decided:** question ids are now `skill:n` for the system-independent skills (count points, points with shape) and `system:skill:n` for the bidding skills, so switching systems keeps point-counting progress and gives each system its own spaced-repetition ladder for bidding. The skill-unlock ladder counts concepts, not systems (16 open-pass hands in any system unlocks the next). `parseQuestionId` still accepts the old `sayc:` prefix. SAYC output is byte-for-byte unchanged (the old tests still pass); only the responder invite/game boundary shifted by one point as a side effect of making it range-aware, still landing 3NT on the pinned 11-opposite-strong-NT test.
- **Verified:** full `npm run check` green; previewed the Acol cheat-sheet and an Acol opening-bid drill at 390px in both modes (page text stays cream over the felt, cards finished in both). Spot-checked the bot: weak-NT responder passes with 10 and bids game with 14, SAYC responder still bids game with 11 opposite a strong NT.
- **Left out:** 2-over-1 (still greyed; it is a third content pack). Acol responses in the bot beyond what SAYC already covered (no Acol-specific strong-two openings; the strong 2♣ is modelled the same simplified way). Acol-specific drills for responding/rebidding — the four skills are shared and only the opening-bid answers differ by system so far.
- **Status:** CI should be green on push.

## 2026-10-22 — Playable hand against bots, with a coach

- **Built:** `features/play/`: four-hand seeded deal; auction with legal-call rules, contract and declarer resolution, and a bot bidder that follows the drill rules (open/pass, opening bid, responses to 1NT and 1-of-a-suit, one rebid, simple overcalls); card play with follow-suit enforcement, trick winners with trumps, bot card choice by beginner rules of thumb (top of sequence, fourth-best, second hand low, third hand high, cheapest winner, draw trumps, ruff when it wins); non-vulnerable duplicate scoring. `useGame` drives bots on a short delay; the human plays South, and both North and South whenever their side declares. `PlayPage`: table with hidden hands as backs, dummy face-up after the lead, trick area, bidding box with only legal bids, Coach card with a Tips toggle (contextual one-liners) and a "Give me a hint" button that highlights the suggested bid or card with a reason; result card with score and Next board. Play tab, "Play a hand" on Today, Tips setting. Header removed (felt runs to the top; sun/moon in Settings). 7 new tests (34 total).
- **Decided:** hint and opponents are the same mind, so the hint never recommends something the bots would not do. No doubles/redoubles, no vulnerability, no Stayman/transfers in the bot yet. Board numbers seed the deal, so a board can be replayed.
- **Left out:** claim, undo, play review, smarter bots, duplicate scoring with vulnerability, saving results. All natural next steps, best done in a fresh session dedicated to the game.
